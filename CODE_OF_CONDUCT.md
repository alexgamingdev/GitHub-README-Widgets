# Code of Conduct

We want GitHub README Widgets to be a welcoming, respectful community for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal traits.
- Accept that people may have different experience levels, backgrounds, and opinions.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy privately to the repository owner through a verified contact method on their GitHub profile. Do not post private information or security-sensitive details in a public issue or discussion.

The maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
